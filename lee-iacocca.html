From Allentown to the Corner Office: The Lee Iacocca Story

Before he was saving Chrysler from bankruptcy or launching the car that defined a generation, Lee Iacocca was just a kid in Allentown, the son of Italian immigrants, watching his father run a hot dog restaurant and one of the country's first car rental agencies. Lehigh County doesn't just claim him as a hometown hero, it built the guy.

From an engineering degree at Lehigh University to the top of two of America's biggest automakers, Iacocca's career reads like an underdog story that happens to be entirely true.

The Story So Far

👶
1924

Born in Allentown

Lee Iacocca is born to Italian immigrant parents, Nicola and Antonietta.

🎓
1942

Allentown High School

Graduates with honors from his hometown high school.

📐
Lehigh University

Industrial Engineering

Earns his engineering degree just down the road in Bethlehem, PA.

🏫
1946

Princeton Master's

Completes a master's degree in mechanical engineering.

🔩
1946

Joins Ford

Starts as an engineer, quickly moves into sales and marketing.

💵
1956

“56 for ’56”

His loan campaign, low down payment, low monthly cost, becomes a sales sensation.

🏎
1964

Mustang Debuts

Iacocca helps launch the Ford Mustang, selling over 400,000 in its first year.

🏢
1970

Ford President

Rises to the top of Ford Motor Company.

🚪
1978

Fired by Ford

Clashes with Henry Ford II and is let go, despite the company's record profits.

🤝
1978-79

Chrysler Rescue

Becomes President, then Chairman of a nearly bankrupt Chrysler Corporation.

💰
1979-83

Loan Guarantee

Secures a $1.5 billion federal loan guarantee, then repays it years ahead of schedule.

🚗
1984

Minivan Launch

Chrysler's minivan reshapes the American family car.

🚪
1992

Retires

Steps down as Chrysler's chairman and CEO.

⭐
2019

An American Legacy

Passes away on July 2, 2019, leaving a business legacy that started on the streets of Allentown.
