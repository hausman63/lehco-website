Bottled Lehigh County Pride: The A-Treat Soda Story

Every Lehigh County kid remembers the taste. Not a national brand, not something you'd find in every corner store across the country, just A-Treat, birch beer especially, ice cold, and unmistakably ours. It started small, in a garage, the way the best local legends usually do.

James Munjone began bottling soda out of his family home on Front Street in Allentown, and what started as a neighborhood hustle grew into one of Lehigh County's most beloved brands, one that survived a century, a shutdown, and a hometown rescue.

The Story So Far

🏠
1918

Born in a Garage

James Munjone starts a beverage business at his Front Street home in Allentown.

🏭
1932

Union Boulevard

Production moves to the East Allentown building that would bottle A-Treat for the next 83 years.

🧃
Mid-1900s

25 Flavors Strong

Birch beer, sarsaparilla, blue raspberry, and more roll off the line, most of them flavors the big brands never touched.

📦
2015

Last Call

The original company closes its doors after nearly a century in business.

🤝
Sep 5, 2015

A Local Rescue

Jaindl Companies relaunches A-Treat, and it's back on Lehigh County shelves within the year.

⭐
Today

Still Local

A-Treat remains the taste of a Lehigh County summer, no substitutes accepted.
