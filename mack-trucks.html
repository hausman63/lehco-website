The Bulldog Was Born Here: The Mack Trucks Story

Every time you see a Mack Truck rumble down the highway with that snarling bulldog on the hood, you're looking at Lehigh County. This isn't a brand that moved here for tax breaks or cheap land. Mack Trucks was born here, built here, and for over a century has been putting Allentown on the map of American industry, one heavy-duty engine at a time.

The bulldog itself has a legend attached, because of course it does. During World War I, British soldiers reportedly gave Mack's AC Model trucks the nickname "Bulldog Mack," a short-hooded, stubby-looking truck that just kept running no matter how badly the front lines beat it up. The name stuck so hard that Mack put an actual bulldog hood ornament on its trucks starting in the 1930s, and it's been snarling at the front of every Mack ever since.
