Privacy Policy

LEHCO Brand LLC ("LEHCO"), Lehigh County, Pennsylvania. Last updated October 28, 2026.

LEHCO makes short videos about the history of Lehigh County and publishes them on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. This policy explains what we do with information when you interact with those videos.

What we collect

When you comment on one of our posts, the platform shows us your public username, your comment and when you posted it. Our own software (the app "LEHCO comments") reads the comments on our own accounts only so we can read them and reply. We do not collect anything else: no private messages, contact lists, location or payment information.

How we use it

  • To read and reply to comments on our videos.
  • To log video ideas and corrections that viewers suggest, so we can credit the viewer by their public username if we make their idea.

We do not sell, rent or share this information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not contact anyone outside the platform.

Photos you email us

If you email old photos to lehcohistory@gmail.com, we keep your email and the photos only to ask for, and keep a record of, your permission. We use a photo in a video only with your written OK, and we credit you the way you choose.

Where it is kept

Comment logs are kept in files on our own computer. We keep them as long as they help us answer viewers and credit ideas.

Deleting your data

To have your comments, username or photos removed from our records, email lehcohistory@gmail.com with your username and the platform. We delete them within 30 days and confirm by email. Deleting a comment on Instagram, YouTube or TikTok itself is done in that app.
