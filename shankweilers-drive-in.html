Still Rolling: Shankweiler's Drive-In, the World's Oldest

Long before streaming, before multiplexes, before Blockbuster even existed, there was Shankweiler's. Tucked into Orefield, this drive-in has been showing movies under the stars since 1934, making it the oldest continuously operating drive-in theater anywhere in the world. Not in Pennsylvania. Not in America. On the planet.

It opened as the second drive-in ever built in the country, and while its predecessor in Camden, New Jersey came and went, Shankweiler's just kept the projector running.

The Story So Far

🎥
Apr 15, 1934

Lights, Camera

Wilson Shankweiler opens Pennsylvania's first drive-in theater in Orefield.

🔊
1948

Sound Arrives

In-car speaker poles are installed, upgrading the movie-night experience.

💨
1955

Rebuilt After the Storm

Hurricane Diane levels the screen; a new CinemaScope screen rises in its place.

📻
1986

Stereo Sound

Shankweiler's becomes the first drive-in to broadcast audio in FM stereo.

🏙
2022

New Owners, New Era

The Moving Picture Cinema takes over and expands the theater to year-round operation.

⭐
Today

Still the Oldest

The world's oldest continuously operating drive-in, still running in Orefield.
