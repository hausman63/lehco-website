Lights in the Parkway: thirty seasons, one missing year.

Allentown's drive-through holiday lights started in 1996. The 2026 show is season 30. Count the years and you get 31. Here is where the other one went.

The whole story in sixty seconds

Season 30 opens Friday, November 27, 2026

The winter the lights left the park.

In June 2015, a stone wall built by WPA workers in the 1930s gave way in heavy rain. That winter the lights went downtown to Hamilton Street instead. They came home in 2016.

How it went

1920s
The Lehigh Parkway is planned along Little Lehigh Creek. Most of it is built between 1936 and 1940 with WPA money, including a stone wall that carries the road in.
1996
The first Lights in the Parkway. The city has counted its seasons from this year ever since.
2010
Season 15. By then the city counted about two million visitors and more than $400,000 raised for local nonprofit groups.
June 20, 2015
A section of the WPA wall on Park Drive, about 50 feet long, collapses in heavy rain.
Winter 2015
The city says wall repairs make it impossible to bring two lanes of traffic into the park safely. The show is replaced by “Allentown Lights” on Hamilton Street, from 10th to 6th. This is the missing year.
2016
The lights come home for the 20th season, opening November 25.
2020
The show closes two nights early, overwhelmed: more than 15,000 vehicles had come through.
December 2025
Vandals wreck displays, including Old Man Winter, 23 feet tall and 45 feet long. The show reopens within days with displays on loan.
2026
Season 30: a quarter-million lights over more than a mile, and a new red, white and blue eagle for the nation's 250th.

If you are going this season

Dates

Hours
