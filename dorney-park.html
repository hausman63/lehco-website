Since 1884: The Dorney Park Story

Before it was a park full of roller coasters, Dorney Park was a trout hatchery. Solomon Dorney built a summer resort around his fish ponds outside Allentown, and somewhere along the way, Lehigh County residents realized this was a really nice place to spend a Saturday. One thing led to another, and 140-plus years later, it's the fifth-oldest amusement park still operating in the entire country.

Through fires, rebuilds, ownership changes, and a centennial celebration, Dorney Park has stayed exactly what it's always been: Lehigh County's park.
