The Store That Built Downtown: Hess's Department Store

Ask anyone in Lehigh County over the age of 40 about Hess's and watch their face change. The Patio restaurant. The Christmas parade down Hamilton Street. A downtown Allentown flagship so central to local life that losing it still stings decades later. For 97 years, Hess's wasn't just a place to shop, it was a Lehigh County institution.

It started with two brothers who saw an opportunity nobody else did, and grew into a regional retail empire before the realities of the mall era caught up with it.

The Story So Far

🛍
1897

Grand Opening

Max and Charles Hess open their first store at 9th and Hamilton in downtown Allentown.

🕊
1922

A Legacy Continues

Max Hess Sr. passes away; brother Charles carries the business forward.

🏗
1927

Eight Stories Tall

A major annex expands the flagship store with new departments and shipping space.

📈
1980s

Peak Hess's

The chain grows to nearly 80 stores at its commercial height.

📉
1994

End of an Era

The remaining 30 stores are sold off to May Department Stores and Bon-Ton.

🔒
Jan 15, 1996

Doors Close for Good

The 9th and Hamilton flagship closes permanently.

⭐
Today

Still Missed

Mention the Patio or the Parade to a Lehigh County native and settle in for a story.
