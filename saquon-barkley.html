From Coplay to the Super Bowl: The Saquon Barkley Story

Before the NFL records and the Super Bowl ring, Saquon Barkley was a kid whose family moved from the Bronx looking for a safer, quieter place to grow up, and found it in Lehigh County. Bethlehem, then Allentown, then Coplay: by the time he suited up for Whitehall High School, Barkley was already outrunning everyone on the field.

What happened next, Penn State records, a top draft pick, a historic season, and a Super Bowl, is the kind of story Lehigh County gets to claim as entirely its own.
